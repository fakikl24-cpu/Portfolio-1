Playing around with Shiny

5/04/2026

This portfolio aims to play around with Shiny apps. In this app, I hope to have the user select a mood and receive a list of songs, video games, youtubers, and/or things to do while they feel like this. The main goal is to make a app that gives me a list of things to do during certain moods. I often forget the things I have access to when I’m in certain moods and this may be a fun tool to use to mitigate that fatigue.

ui <- fluidPage(
  titlePanel("Mood-Based Recommendations"),
  
  sidebarLayout(
    sidebarPanel(
      selectInput("mood", "How are you feeling?", 
                  choices = c("Bed-Rotting", "Irritated", "Hyper", "Chill", "Anxious"))
    ),
    
    mainPanel(
      h3("Recommendations for you:"),
      uiOutput("recommendations")
    )
  )
)

While very basic, this app shows the extent of shiny app’s capabilities! Using this app, I can select my mood and see my past self’s recommendations for what to do, listen to, watch, or play while I feel that mood. I can also come back to this in the future and add more moods later or update the app with more recommendations in the future.
